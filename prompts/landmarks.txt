You design low-poly buildings for ARA Driving, a bright, toy-like browser driving game. Each building is a list of simple solid shapes. A player drives past it and must tell at a glance what kind of place it is.

Answer with ONLY a JSON array. Each entry:
{"id":"cafe_striped_awning","kind":"cafe","parts":[{"shape":"box","x":0,"y":0,"z":-1,"w":10,"h":5,"l":8,"color":"#f2e3c6"}]}
Coordinates, in metres:
- x is left and right, z is front and back, and the road is in front at +z. Put doors, signs and awnings on the +z face.
- x and z give the centre of a part. y gives its BASE, so a part of height h on top of a part with y 0 and height 5 has y 5.
- w is the size along x, h the height, l the size along z.
- shape is box, cone or cylinder. Cones and cylinders use w and l as their width.
Rules the checker enforces:
- 6 to 16 parts.
- Everything fits inside the footprint: abs(x) + w/2 and abs(z) + l/2 must each be at most 7.
- Everything fits between y 0 and y 20. Every size is at least 0.2.
- One main body has y exactly 0, with w and l of at least 3.
- Nothing floats: every part touches a part that leads down to the ground. Compute the y of each part from what it rests on. Poles under awnings or signs are good.
- color is lowercase #rrggbb, with at least 3 different colours. Use cheerful, slightly soft colours.
What makes a good one: a strong silhouette and one telling feature. School: clock tower or flag. Park: gate, cone trees, a bench. Clinic: white walls and a green cross. Library: columns and steps. Market: striped stalls. Cafe: awning and tables. Depot: big roller door and crates. Office: tall with rows of window strips. House: pitched roof, chimney. Shop: big window and a sign.
