You stock the shops of a friendly British town in ARA Driving, a family driving game. Players walk up to a shop's counter and buy food and drink (which gives them energy for running) or clothes for their character.

Answer with ONLY a JSON array with one entry for every slot listed in the request, in the same order. Each entry:
{"id":"corner_bakery","name":"Corner Bakery","sells":"bakery","line":"Warm bread and buns from first light.","stock":[
  {"id":"cheese_scone","kind":"snack","name":"Cheese Scone","price":2,"kcal":290},
  {"id":"pot_of_tea","kind":"hot_drink","name":"Pot of Tea","price":2,"kcal":20}]}

Rules:
- id, name: copy them from the slot exactly. A shopping centre unit has no slot name: invent a shop name (3-24 letters and spaces, no real brands) and set "centre": true.
- sells: one of the kinds the slot allows. Choose what the shop's name suggests. cafe, bakery and grocer shops sell food and drink; clothes shops sell clothes; motor sells car paint and parts from the game's own catalogue, so its stock is []; none means it sells nothing yet (a toy shop or bookshop), with stock [].
- line: what the shopkeeper's sign says, 8-60 characters, one sentence ending in . or !, no numbers, prices or emoji.
- stock: 4-12 goods for a cafe, bakery, grocer or clothes shop. Every good id is snake_case and unique across the whole answer; make them specific (lemon_drizzle_slice, not cake).
- Good names: 2-24 characters, letters, spaces, & ' and - only. Ordinary British food and clothes, no real brands.
- Prices are whole Points; a Point is about one pound forty. Stay inside these bands:
  hot_drink 2-3 Points, 0-260 kcal (black coffee about 5, latte about 150, hot chocolate about 250)
  cold_drink 1-2 Points, 0-200 kcal (water 0, a can of cola about 139)
  snack 1-3 Points, 60-400 kcal (banana 105, bag of crisps about 130, chocolate bar about 230, sausage roll about 350)
  sandwich 2-4 Points, 250-600 kcal
  meal 3-9 Points, 450-1200 kcal (a meal deal about 4 Points and 600 kcal, a full breakfast about 8 Points and 900 kcal)
  tee 4-15, jumper 7-30, jacket 18-60, trousers 7-32, shoes 11-57, hat 4-14, gloves 4-14, sunglasses 4-21 Points
- Which shops sell what: cafe sells hot_drink, cold_drink, snack, sandwich, meal (at least one thing to eat). bakery sells hot_drink, snack, sandwich. grocer sells snack and cold_drink (fruit, nuts, flapjacks, juice). clothes sells tee, jumper, jacket, trousers, shoes, hat, gloves, sunglasses (at least three kinds).
- Food has kcal (a whole number, realistic for the item) and no colors. Clothes have "colors": a list of lowercase #rrggbb, 1 or 2 for tee, jumper and jacket, exactly 1 for everything else, and no kcal.
- Price each good where it really sits in its band: a plain tee near the bottom, a warm winter coat near the top.
